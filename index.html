Document Password
The password is the decryption key, not a check the page performs. The document URL is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from it and left out of the link, the QR code and the generated file — so there is nothing to read without it, and nothing to recover it from if you lose it. Send it to viewers over a different channel than the link.
